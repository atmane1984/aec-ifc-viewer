# AEC IFC Viewer privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

AEC IFC Viewer is published by Atmane Laouati.

## Model files

AEC IFC Viewer reads the IFC files you choose and processes them on your
device. The app does not upload your model files to the publisher or to any
cloud service. Viewing and inspecting a model does not change the original file.

## Local settings

The app stores its theme preference on your device. Windows may remember the
folder you last used in the file-open dialog. These settings stay on your
device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Accounts, advertising and analytics

The app does not require an account, does not show advertising and does not
include an analytics or telemetry service. Microsoft Store and Windows may
separately process installation, update and diagnostic information under
Microsoft's own privacy terms.

## Clipboard

If you choose Copy GUID, the app places the selected identifier on the Windows
clipboard. Windows and other applications may access clipboard content
according to your system settings.

## Support and feedback

Bug reports and feature requests are handled through this repository's public
[Issues](../../issues) page. Anything you post there, including attachments, is
visible to everyone. Do not post confidential model files or personal
information. If a problem only happens with a confidential model, describe it
or share a reduced model you are allowed to make public.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new
"Last updated" date.
