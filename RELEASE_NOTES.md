# AEC IFC Viewer release notes

## 1.0.0 (initial release)

- Open IFC files and browse projects, sites, buildings, storeys and elements.
- Orbit, pan and zoom; view cube and camera presets.
- Select elements in the 3D view or the model tree and inspect their IFC
  attributes, classification, relations and STEP data.
- Search by name, type, description, GUID or element ID.
- Hide, show, isolate and fit elements or spatial groups.
- Feature edges and a movable section plane.
- Light, dark or system appearance.
- Five sample models included.
